The Story

What drives a Mad Doctor mad? Is it obsession… or irresistible allure?

Enter the strange and seductive world of Dr. Johanny Porkenpie — a brilliant but unhinged physician whose experiments blur the line between science, desire, and theatrical illusion. Inside his shadowy laboratory—part cabaret, part dreamscape—he conducts weekly performances that are as captivating as they are unpredictable.

As he attempts to resurrect his long-lost love, audiences are drawn into a spellbinding journey of Love, Death, and temptation. Each act unfolds with dramatic flair, playful humor, and dazzling burlesque artistry that leaves guests both laughing and leaning in for more.
