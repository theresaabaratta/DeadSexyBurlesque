About the Production

Where theatrical storytelling meets elite burlesque performance.

A New York–Born Experience

Dead Sexy is more than a stage show—it’s a carefully crafted theatrical production developed in the heart of New York City. Drawing from one of the most competitive and creative performance scenes in the world, the show brings together a cast of highly trained performers who specialize in movement, storytelling, and burlesque artistry.

Casting takes place in NYC, where performers are selected not only for their stage presence, but for their ability to embody character, emotion, and precision. Each cast member brings a unique background—from dance and theater to cabaret and performance art—creating a layered and dynamic experience for audiences.

Training & Preparation

Behind every performance is a deep commitment to rehearsal, timing, and craft.

Rehearsed Precision

Every act is choreographed and refined through extensive rehearsal sessions, ensuring each moment on stage feels seamless and intentional.

Character Development

Performers are trained to fully step into their roles, blending theatrical storytelling with expressive movement and stage presence.

Performance Artistry

From timing and pacing to audience engagement, the cast is prepared to deliver a show that feels both polished and alive.

Our Vision

The goal of Dead Sexy is to elevate burlesque into a fully immersive theatrical experience. By combining professional casting, disciplined training, and a bold creative vision, the production offers something that goes beyond traditional nightlife entertainment.

Each performance is designed to feel cinematic, engaging, and memorable—whether you’re attending a ticketed show or booking a private event. The rotating cast ensures that no two performances are exactly the same, while maintaining the same high standard of quality and energy.
